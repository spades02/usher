Your support chatbot knows your product. Your customer's own assistant knows
*them*: their preferences, their history, what they tried yesterday. Usher
gives that assistant a safe, standard way into your app.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Define tools once" icon="seti:typescript">
    Typed actions like `list_orders` or `cancel_order`, with the schema
    library you already use. Usher serves them over MCP to every assistant.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Consent your customers understand" icon="approve-check">
    An OAuth 2.1 server wired into your existing login. Customers tick
    exactly which tools an agent may use, and can change it at any time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Guardrails built in" icon="warning">
    Irreversible actions wait for the customer's approval on your site. Audit
    log, rate limits, a kill switch, and replay detection for stolen tokens.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Runs in your app" icon="laptop">
    A library, not a hosted service. Your database, your domain, your data.
    Next.js, Express, Hono, SvelteKit, or any backend via the standalone
    server.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
